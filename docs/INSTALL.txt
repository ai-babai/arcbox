Arcbox 0.2.3
macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

INSTALL

Drag Arcbox.app to the Applications shortcut, then launch it from Applications.
For the ZIP download, unzip it and move Arcbox.app to /Applications yourself.
No Homebrew, Swift, or separate archive engine is required on the receiving Mac.

FIRST LAUNCH

This early release is signed ad hoc, not with an Apple Developer ID, and has
not been notarized by Apple. macOS may block the first launch.

If you trust the download and have verified its SHA-256:
1. Try to open Arcbox once.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Find the blocked Arcbox entry and choose Open Anyway; authenticate if asked.

Do not disable Gatekeeper or other system protections globally.
On a managed Mac your administrator may need to approve the app.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445

USE

Open: select an archive or any volume. Arcbox finds the rest.
Create: choose files, format, and optionally Split into parts / Разделить на части.
Extract: choose the output folder. Arcbox reads all parts automatically.
English by default. Switch EN / RU in the toolbar; View > Language also offers System.
System dialog chrome follows macOS.

Sidebar: the button at the left of the toolbar or Control-Command-S.
Agent tools: View/Вид > Инструменты агента, or Shift-Command-K.
CLI: /Applications/Arcbox.app/Contents/MacOS/arcbox --help

REMOVE

Quit Arcbox and move /Applications/Arcbox.app to Trash.
Your archives and extracted files are ordinary files and remain where you saved them.
